# ADR 0001: Record architecture decisions

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Architecture decisions are easy to lose in chat, pull requests and commits.
Without a record, a later reader cannot tell why a choice was made, which
alternatives were rejected, or what would make the decision wrong.

## Decision

Every decision that changes a public interface, a data model, a dependency, a
security boundary or an operational procedure gets an ADR under `docs/adr/`.
The ADR states the context, the options, the decision and its consequences.

## Consequences

- The architecture has a reviewable trail.
- Superseded decisions stay in the repository and are marked as superseded.
- An ADR is not a design document; it is one decision and its reasons.
